How a roadmap works

I

Checkpoints

A roadmap is a road of checkpoints. Reach each one in order and you have covered the ground.

II

Resources

Every checkpoint holds free resources, links and PDFs, each with a note on why it is worth your time.

III

Branches

When a checkpoint has two or more resources, the road forks. Take whichever suits you; the paths rejoin at the next checkpoint.

Know a road others should walk?

Anyone can contribute a roadmap. Submissions are read by our stewards before they are published, so every road on the map has been checked.
